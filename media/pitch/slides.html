The gap

Stock Tokens trade 24/7. Their price feeds run 24/5.

Chainlink's tokenized-equity feeds publish through the overnight session, then go quiet from Friday 20:00 ET to Sunday 20:00 ET. The Uniswap pools for RHTSLA, RHNVDA and RHAMZN never close.

The product

Buy protection. Or write it and earn the premium.

Protect

Pick TSLA · strike 90% · expiry "Monday open". Pay a stablecoin premium. Receive an ERC-1155 position paying max(strike − settle, 0) per share.

Earn

Deposit stablecoins into a per-stock ERC-4626 vault. Every premium lands in the vault and lifts the share price. Withdraw any unlocked liquidity.

Pricing · Arbitrum Stylus

The premium is computed onchain, in Rust.

  • Walks the feed's Chainlink round history with getRoundData
  • Realized volatility annualized over open-market seconds, so the freeze doesn't dilute it
  • Closed seconds are charged at m × σ: the weekend has a price
σeff² · T = σ² · ( Topen + m² · Tclosed )

Black-Scholes put on σeff, plus writer spread, floored at intrinsic. 15 KB of WASM, ~150 lines of i128 fixed-point math, native unit tests. Painful and gas-hungry in Solidity; natural in Rust.

Settlement

The first print after expiry. Not a convenient one later.

Rule

settle() walks back the round history to the earliest print at or after expiry. A "Monday open" put settles on the Monday open.

Pause-aware

If the feed's oraclePaused() is set for a corporate action, settlement waits.

Never stranded

If no post-expiry print arrives within the grace window, the last price is used so collateral is always released.
